Forest of Undead

Game Overview

Forest of Undead is a fast-paced survival shooter where the objective is not completion but endurance. Players grow in power through upgrade decisions while enemy pressure escalates endlessly, creating inevitable failure driven by build choices rather than randomness.

Screenshots

Mid-Game Horde Combat

Player faces dense enemy waves with stacked upgrades, showcasing readable chaos and build progression under pressure.

Card-Based Upgrade Choice

On level-up, players choose one of three randomized upgrade cards, shaping each run into a unique build.

Pet Summon & Companion

A fireball-shooting pet follows the player and scales through its own three-tier upgrade path.

Player Death & Run End

Health depletion triggers a clear death animation, reinforcing the endless loop of survive → upgrade → fail → retry.

Core Gameplay Loop

Spawn Enemies → Defeat Enemies → Gain EXP → Level Up → Choose 1 of 3 Upgrades → Difficulty Scales → Repeat Until Death

Key Systems

Upgrade System

The game uses stat-driven randomized upgrades to ensure scalability, balance clarity, and replayability. Different upgrade combinations naturally form distinct build archetypes such as area control, summoner, and glass cannon.

Enemy Design

Difficulty increases through controlled stat progression rather than complex AI behavior. This maintains performance stability and visual readability even during high-intensity enemy swarms.

Postmortem

What Went Right

  • Complete gameplay loop shipped within scope
  • Strong replayability through upgrade randomness
  • Clear system scalability

What Could Improve

  • Limited enemy behavior variety
  • No meta-progression between runs
