Overview

Game Overview

Forest of Undead is a 2D top-down survival shooter inspired by endless survival games like Vampire Survivors. The player is placed inside a cursed forest and must survive increasingly intense waves of undead enemies by continuously moving, fighting, and upgrading.

The game emphasizes long-term survival over completion. Failure is inevitable — the goal is to survive longer each run by making better upgrade decisions and adapting to escalating enemy pressure. The upgrade system is stat-driven and randomized, creating distinct build archetypes every playthrough without hand-authored content.

Gameplay

Core Loop

Spawn Enemies → Defeat Enemies → Gain EXP → Level Up → Choose 1 of 3 Upgrades → Difficulty Scales → Repeat Until Death

As the loop progresses, enemy spawn rates increase, enemy types expand, and player upgrade decisions compound into stronger or weaker builds — reinforcing learning through failure.

Systems

Upgrade System & Build Expression

Players choose 1 of 3 randomized upgrades on level-up. All upgrades are stat-driven for rapid iteration and clear cause-effect relationships. Their combinations naturally form distinct build archetypes.

💣

Area Control — Bombs, Tornadoes, Scythes

Clears dense clusters. Tornadoes force enemies into predictable paths. Orbiting scythes provide passive crowd control. Best combined for maximum space denial.

🔫

Primary DPS — Gun & Speed Upgrades

Bullet count, damage, reload speed, and travel speed upgrades. High-risk direct combat builds that scale sharply but demand precise positioning.

👻

Summoner Build — Indirect Damage

Summon entities that fire fireballs autonomously. Combined with speed and health upgrades for consistent background damage with reduced player input burden.

❤️

Survivability — Heart Upgrades

Instant heal-to-max and max health increase. Allows recovery from mistakes and extends run longevity. The difference between a failed run and a dominant one.

Reflection

Postmortem

What Went Right

  • Complete gameplay loop shipped within scope and timeline
  • High replayability through upgrade randomness and build variety
  • Clear system scalability — stat-driven design easy to extend
  • Visual clarity maintained during high-intensity enemy swarms

What Could Improve

  • Limited enemy behavior variety — scaling is purely stat-based
  • No meta-progression system between runs
  • Additional enemy modifiers would add depth without AI complexity
