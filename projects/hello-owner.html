Game Overview

Hello Owner places the player in an abandoned warehouse for a late-night job unloading parcels. What begins as routine labor slowly escalates into psychological horror as the environment reacts, restricts movement, and tests obedience. Player choices and exploration paths determine multiple endings.

Design Pillars

Normalcy Before Horror

The experience begins grounded and believable. Horror emerges gradually through subtle environmental disruptions rather than immediate threats.

Environment as an Antagonist

The warehouse actively reacts to the player by blocking paths, reconfiguring space, and enforcing movement rules.

Maze as Psychological Pressure

The maze is a rule-based test, not a puzzle. Lighting constraints, enforced stillness, and moving racks strip player agency and increase psychological tension.

No Combat, Only Consequences

The player cannot fight back. Progress depends on awareness, restraint, and understanding environmental rules.

Multiple Endings

Player behavior, exploration, and compliance determine which ending unfolds. Each ending reinforces the themes of ownership, greed, and escape.

Postmortem

What Went Right

  • Strong atmosphere with minimal mechanics
  • Completed end-to-end as a solo project
  • Clear narrative-driven structure

What Could Improve

  • More mid-game interaction variety
  • Expanded environmental consequences
