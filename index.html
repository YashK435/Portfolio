How I build games

01

Define the experience

Start with what the player should feel at the end of a session, then work backwards.

02

Prototype the core loop

Build early to test mechanics, pacing, and clarity — not to polish.

03

Iterate until it feels right

Refine mechanics and progression to match the intended player experience.

04

Finish and ship

Stability, clarity, and a complete playable build — every time.
