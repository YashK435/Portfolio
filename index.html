About Me

I am a game developer focused on building complete, playable experiences from start to finish. My work covers the full development cycle, including concept definition, gameplay design, implementation, iteration, and final delivery.

I begin each project by defining the experience I want the player to have at the end of a session, and then design the mechanics, progression, and structure needed to support that goal. The approach is chosen based on what best serves the player and the intended experience, rather than following a fixed design style.

I currently work primarily in Unreal Engine, using Blueprints and C++ to design, implement, and iterate on gameplay logic, interactions, and level behavior as a solo developer. I place strong emphasis on finishing projects and refining the player experience before release.

My long-term goal is to create games that engage and amuse players, whether through progression, atmosphere, or carefully paced experiences.

How I Make Games

1. Define the Experience

I start by deciding what the player should feel or understand by the end of a play session.

2. Prototype Core Gameplay

I build early gameplay prototypes to test mechanics, pacing, and player clarity.

3. Build a Playable Structure

The prototype is expanded into a complete gameplay loop or vertical slice.

4. Iterate and Refine

Mechanics and progression are refined to better match the intended player experience.

5. Polish and Finish

I focus on stability, clarity, and delivery of a finished, playable build.
